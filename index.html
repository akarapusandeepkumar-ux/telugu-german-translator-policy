Privacy Policy

Telugu German Translator

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Telugu German Translator app ("the App") handles information when you use it.

Information We Use

The App does not require you to create an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email, or phone number.

The App uses the following on your device, only when you actively use a related feature:

  • Microphone: Used only when you tap the microphone icon to convert your speech to text. Audio is processed for that single request and is not stored by the App.
  • Text you type or speak: Sent securely to Google's Cloud Translation API to generate a translation. This text is subject to Google's own privacy practices for that service.
  • Photos (optional): If you choose to upload a photo to replace the default tutor avatar, that photo is stored only on your own device and is never uploaded to any server.
  • Translation history: Your past translations are saved locally on your device only, so you can view them later, including without an internet connection. This history is never transmitted anywhere and can be cleared by uninstalling the App.

Third-Party Services

The App uses Google's Cloud Translation API to perform translations. When you request a translation, the text is sent to Google for processing. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details on how Google handles this data.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties, other than the translation processing described above, which is required for the App's core function.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App is a general-purpose translation tool suitable for all ages.

Data Security

Your translation history and any custom avatar photo are stored only locally on your device. We do not operate our own servers that store your personal data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
